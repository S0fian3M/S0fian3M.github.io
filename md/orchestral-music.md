# orchestral music
I have played with a variety of orchestras. Since 2024, I have been part of the Choir and Orchestra of Sorbonne University. I have also played with Levallois-Peret's harmony orchestra, and won second place at the 2025 French National Harmony Orchestra Championship.

Some recordings of what I played with orchestras include:
- [Carmina Burana (Orff)](https://youtu.be/AIUV9ODnG_E) (Timpani)
- [Les Djinns (Fauré)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gzZ7_plHMbA) (Timpani)
- [Dionysiaques (Schmitt)](https://youtu.be/DaMZq_DddkA?t=3086) (Hand cymbals, tambourine, suspended cymbals)
